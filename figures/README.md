# figures/ — the six published figures

All six are regenerated by `../analysis/plot_figures.py`, which writes into this directory. The plotting script carries transcribed values rather than reading the result files, so where a figure and a table in the article disagree, the table is authoritative.

```bash
python3 ../analysis/plot_figures.py              # all six
python3 ../analysis/plot_figures.py --only curve # one
```

---

### `validity-vs-concurrency.png`

![Schema validity against concurrency](validity-vs-concurrency.png)

The headline figure: schema validity against concurrency, all four arms on one axis. Strict self-hosted arms flat at 1.00, prompt-only flat at 0.67, managed endpoint degrading from 1.00 to about 0.85. Three flat lines and one that droops.

The 0.67 line is a property of that task set rather than of unenforced generation generally, since two of three templates always succeed. The five-step workload runs the same prompt-only arm at 1.00.

### `truncation-repair-by-completion.png`

![Repair outcomes by document completion](truncation-repair-by-completion.png)

Parse, schema, and business-rule checks all reach 1.00 by 60% completion while byte-identical stays near zero. Array-of-objects stays at 0.00 through nine deciles, because `minItems` plus required per-record fields reject a half-written record immediately.

Past 60% every automated check says yes and none of the documents is the one the model was writing.

### `backend-conformance-grid.png`

![Conformance grid of 18 schema keywords](backend-conformance-grid.png)

Eighteen schema keywords across xgrammar and guidance. xgrammar's three untyped fragments are the only silent failures anywhere in the grid; guidance had none. For typed keywords both backends are honest: everything accepted was enforced, everything unsupported was refused with an error.

### `failure-relocation-ladder.png`

![The validation ladder per arm](failure-relocation-ladder.png)

The same task fails at the parse rung without enforcement and at semantic validation with it. Both arms yield three of four usable tasks: enforcement moved the failure rather than removing it.

### `latency-vs-validity-sustained.png`

![Latency and queue depth against concurrency](latency-vs-validity-sustained.png)

p99 climbs from 2.3 to 23.6 seconds and queue depth reaches 272, while schema validity stays flat at 1.00. Contention moved latency by an order of magnitude and validity not at all.

### `grammar-cache-cardinality.png`

![Grammar cache and cardinality, two panels](grammar-cache-cardinality.png)

Two panels. Left: TTFT flat at 0.49 s across 16 to 2,048 distinct schemas on the default cache, and flat at 1.23 s on a 1 MiB cache. Once the cache stops fitting the working set, adding schemas changes nothing, because every request is already paying. Right: latency, per-token decode and throughput all move by 2 to 3.6× while schema validity stays at 1.00.

Every rung of the validation ladder reports success across that entire range.
